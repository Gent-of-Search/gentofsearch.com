You Can't Grow Your Sales Pipeline by Just Publishing Content.

Publishing more pages used to be enough to drive visibility for B2B SaaS companies. Ship the pages, get them indexed, watch traffic climb. For years that was the playbook, and it worked.
Not anymore.

Most B2B SaaS companies are now stuck in the same loop. The content engine is running — a writer, an agency, eight posts a month — but organic traffic is flat and the pipeline coming out of it is worse. Core updates get blamed. AI search gets blamed, sometimes fairly. A vague “technical issue” gets blamed. So the team does the only thing it knows how to do. It publishes more. And nothing changes.

The problem is rarely the volume of content.

It's that nobody has connected the strategy, the technical foundations and the implementation into a single plan that actually ships. The audit sits in a Google Doc. Engineering is heads-down on the product roadmap, so SEO tickets never make the sprint. Content lands on a marketing site that can't support it. The work happens. The growth doesn't.

That's the gap I close.

I work with B2B SaaS companies whose SEO channel has stalled — from fast-growing marketing sites to enterprise platforms carrying docs, changelogs, programmatic pages and a decade of legacy URLs.

I don't hand over a document and leave. I diagnose what's actually holding growth back, build a prioritised plan around it, and work alongside your dev, product and content teams until it's live.

If your content engine is running but organic still isn't growing, more content won't fix it. What fixes it is a senior pair of hands who can find the real constraint and stay on it until your team has shipped the solution.

That's where I come in.

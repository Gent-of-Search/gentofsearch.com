<h2>⚡ 9 Takeaways for Driving Traffic with Internal Links</h2>
<p>Below are 9 takeaways from the case studies and studies listed in this article:</p>
<ol>
  <li><strong>🔗 Adding internal links drives SEO traffic</strong> - Studies have shown that pages with more internal links gain more SEO traffic, showing that optimising a website's link architecture is key.</li>
  <li><strong>🧠 Link with purpose</strong> - It is important to link with purpose; SEO case studies show companies don't just increase the number of internal links to important pages without a plan.</li>
  <li><strong>🔢 Anchor text variation</strong> - Studies of millions of internal links show that pages with anchor text variations correlate with more Google search traffic.</li>
  <li><strong>🖱️ Reduce click-depth</strong> - Reducing click-depth from popular pages on a website is key to driving SEO traffic.</li>
  <li><strong>🎈 Reducing internal link bloat</strong> - Reducing unnecessary and low-value internal links on page templates is important.</li>
  <li><strong>🧑‍💻 Server-side render internal links</strong> - Many case studies show the importance of ensuring internal links can be found in the raw HTML on large websites (and not just rendered client-side).</li>
  <li><strong>📈 Internal links from popular pages</strong> - Studies have shown that it is important to add relevant internal links to popular pages that are getting traffic and external links.</li>
  <li><strong>⚔️ Cross-linking between relevant pages</strong> - Many case studies have shown that cross-linking between relevant pages and hubs is a quick win to drive SEO traffic to destination and donor pages.</li>
  <li><strong>📃 Combining internal linking with content strategy</strong> - Combining internal link strategy with content strategy to craft page templates that automatically use internal link best practices, is a powerful way to link with purpose at scale.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Note:</strong> As with any website change, I highly recommend testing any changes you make using SEO split testing tools, like <a href="https://seotesting.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">SEOTesting.com</a> and <a href="https://www.searchpilot.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">SearchPilot.com</a>.</p>
